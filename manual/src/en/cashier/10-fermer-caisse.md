## 10. Register Closing & Z-Report {#fermer-caisse}

At shift or day end:

1. Open **Close Cash Session** (<kbd>F7</kbd>).
2. Count the physical cash in the drawer and enter the counted amount.
3. The system compares it with the expected total and calculates any cash gap.
4. Confirm closing to print the official **Z-Report** and reset the shift.
