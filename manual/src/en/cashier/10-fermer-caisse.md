## 10. Drawer Closing and Z-Report {#fermer-caisse}

At the end of your shift or during a staff handover, you must officially close your cash register session to finalize accounting totals and verify physical cash drawer accuracy.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never leave the store without printing your official **Closing Z-Report**. This document serves as the certified proof of your daily takings for the store owner and accountant.

---

### Steps to Close the Cash Drawer

1. Open the **Drawer Closing** menu or press the shortcut <kbd>F7</kbd>.
2. Physically open the cash drawer and count all currency:
   - Group banknotes by denomination (`2 000`, `1 000`, `500 DZD`) and count metallic coins (`200`, `100`, `50 DZD`).
3. Enter the **Actual Counted Cash** in the designated field.
4. Click **Check Cash Discrepancy**:
   - The system contrasts your counted total against the **Expected Theoretical Total** (Starting float + Cash sales + Cash inflows - Cash outflows).
   - **Zero gap (0 DZD):** Drawer is perfectly balanced.
   - **Positive gap (Surplus):** There is more cash in the drawer than accounted for.
   - **Negative gap (Shortage):** Cash is missing from the drawer.
5. If an unexpected gap appears, recount your drawer carefully and ensure all cash in/out movements were logged.
6. If the discrepancy persists, enter an explanatory comment in the notes field.
7. Click **Confirm Daily Closing (Print Z-Report)**.

---

### The Z-Report

Upon confirming the closing:
1. The thermal printer immediately generates the **Official Z-Report**, detailing:
   - Date, time, and cashier user ID.
   - Gross and net revenue for the shift.
   - Breakdown by payment method (Total Cash, Total TPE, Total BaridiMob, Total Cheques, Total Credit Sales).
   - Detailed VAT and fiscal stamp duty collected.
   - Final cash discrepancy recorded.
   - Total number of tickets issued and average basket value.
2. Staple the Z-Report together with all petty cash vouchers (<kbd>Alt + M</kbd>) and bank TPE debit card receipts.
3. Hand the package to the store manager or deposit it in the store safe.
