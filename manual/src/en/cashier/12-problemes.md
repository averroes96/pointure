## 12. Troubleshooting Common Issues {#problemes}

- **Printer not printing:** Check USB and power cables, paper roll orientation (thermal side down), and run a test print in Settings → Printers.
- **Scanner not entering items:** Ensure the scanner sends an Enter key suffix after scanning.
- **Clock Warning Banner:** Sync your system time using the *Recaler l'horloge* button in Settings → Desktop.
