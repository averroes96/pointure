# Cashier User Guide — Point of Sale {#guide-caisse}

This guide is designed for cashiers and sales clerks operating ShoeDZ at retail footwear store counters. It walks you through every daily task step by step, from opening your cash drawer float to the end-of-day closing (Z-Report).

---

## 1. System Login {#connexion}

To begin your cashier shift, sign in using your personal credentials.

> [!NOTE]
> Never work under a colleague's account: every sale, discount, or cash drawer movement is recorded in the audit log under the active user's identity.

### Login Steps

1. On the main sign-in screen, enter your **mobile phone number** (10 digits starting with `05`, `06`, or `07`, e.g., `0555 12 34 56`) or your registered email address.
2. Enter your **confidential password**.
3. Click **Sign In** (or press the <kbd>Enter</kbd> key).
4. If your retail business operates multiple branches or warehouses, ensure the active branch matches your current store location.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** *Sign-in screen showing mobile phone and password inputs.*

### Changing Your Password or Account Access

To update your password or account permissions, ask your **Store Owner** or **Manager**, who can edit your user profile under **Settings → Users**.

To sign out at the end of your shift, click the sign-out button in the top right corner of the screen.
