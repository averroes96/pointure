1. Introduction

1.1 What is ShoeDZ?

ShoeDZ (also branded as Pointure) is a cloud-based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system purpose-built for Algerian shoe retailers. It consolidates every operation a modern shoe shop needs into a single, web-accessible application — from ringing up sales at the counter to tracking stock across multiple branches, managing wholesale invoicing, and monitoring outstanding client debts.

Because ShoeDZ is multi-tenant, each business gets its own isolated data environment. Your products, clients, transactions, and reports are completely private to your organisation.

Key capabilities at a glance:

  • Point of Sale — Fast barcode-driven checkout with split-payment support.
  • Inventory — Product catalogue with EU-size variants, barcodes, and branch-level stock.
  • Invoicing — Professional wholesale invoicing with TVA, PDF export, and credit notes.
  • Clients & Receivables — Ledger, debt ageing, and cheque tracking.
  • Suppliers & Purchasing — Purchase orders and receiving workflow.
  • Reports — Daily summaries, sales analytics, and stock valuation.
  • Multi-branch — Manage multiple shop locations from one account.

1.2 Who is ShoeDZ for?

ShoeDZ is designed for three types of users inside a retail shoe business:

Role Typical Person Primary Tasks
Owner Business owner / director Full access to all modules including cost prices, profit margins, audit log, billing, and user management.
Manager Shop manager / supervisor All operational tasks; manages users and branches. Cost visibility is configurable. Cannot change billing.
Cashier Sales assistant / cashier POS sales only. Cannot see purchase prices, profit margins, reports, or administrative settings.
If you are a cashier and you cannot see a menu item described in this manual, it is most likely restricted to your role. Contact your manager or owner if you need broader access.

1.3 How to Access the Application

ShoeDZ runs entirely in a modern web browser — no software installation is required on most computers.

Cloud (SaaS) Access

  1. Open any modern browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari).
  2. Navigate to the ShoeDZ web address provided by your administrator or during sign-up.
  3. You will be presented with the login screen.

Local / On-Premise Installation

  1. Your IT administrator will provide you with the internal server address (e.g., http://192.168.1.10:8000) or a local domain name.
  2. Ensure you are connected to the same local network as the server.
  3. Open the address in a browser. The application behaves identically to the cloud version.
ShoeDZ is optimised for desktop and tablet screens. On small smartphone screens some tables may require horizontal scrolling. For best performance at the point of sale, use a desktop or laptop browser.
Always use HTTPS (the padlock icon in your browser's address bar) when accessing ShoeDZ over the internet. If your IT provider has deployed the app on HTTP only within a local network, that is acceptable for an isolated LAN, but never enter your credentials over plain HTTP on a public network.

3. Point of Sale (POS)

The Point of Sale (POS) module is the heart of ShoeDZ for day-to-day retail operations. It is designed for speed — a cashier should be able to complete a typical transaction in under 30 seconds.

3.1 Opening the POS

  1. From the left sidebar, click New Sale.
  2. The POS screen loads with an empty cart on the right and a product search panel on the left.
  3. If your business has multiple branches, verify the branch selector in the top bar shows your current location. Stock is drawn from the selected branch.
You can also press F2 at any time while the POS is open to instantly focus the product search field — useful when your hands are on the keyboard or barcode scanner.

3.2 Searching for Products

ShoeDZ supports two methods of finding a product to add to the cart:

Search by Name

  1. Click in the search box at the top of the product panel (or press F2).
  2. Start typing the product name, brand, or reference. Results appear below the search box after you type two or more characters.
  3. Click on a product from the results list to select it. A variant selection panel will appear (see Section 3.4).

Search by Barcode (Scanner or Manual)

  1. Ensure the search box is focused (press F2 if needed).
  2. Scan the barcode on the product with a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner, or manually type the barcode number.
  3. If the barcode string is 8 or more digits long and corresponds to a known variant, ShoeDZ automatically adds one unit of that variant to the cart immediately — no further selection is needed. This is the fastest path for checkout.
  4. If the barcode is fewer than 8 digits, the system treats the input as a text search.
When using a barcode scanner, make sure the scanner is configured to append an Enter keystroke after the barcode data. This is the default for most retail scanners and ensures the auto-add behaviour fires correctly.
If a scanned barcode is not found, ShoeDZ will display a "Product not found" message. Do not continue scanning other items — check that the variant's barcode has been correctly assigned in the product catalogue. See Section 4.2.

3.3 Keyboard Shortcuts

The following keyboard shortcuts are available within the POS screen to speed up operations:

3.4 Selecting a Variant

After selecting a product by name (not via barcode scan), a variant selector panel appears. Each product can have multiple variants — combinations of EU size and colour.

  1. In the variant panel, locate the Size grid (EU sizes 28–47). Sizes with no available stock are greyed out and cannot be selected.
  2. Click the desired EU size button.
  3. If the product is available in multiple colours at that size, colour swatches or colour name buttons appear. Click the desired colour.
  4. Adjust the Quantity field. It defaults to 1. You can type directly or use the + / − buttons. You cannot enter a quantity greater than available stock.
  5. Click Add to Cart (or press Enter). The line appears in the cart on the right.
If you attempt to add a quantity greater than available stock, ShoeDZ will show a validation error. If you need to override this (e.g., you know a shipment is incoming), contact your manager to perform a stock adjustment first.

3.5 Managing the Cart

The cart panel on the right side of the POS screen lists all items added to the current transaction. The following operations are available per line item:

Changing Quantity

  1. Click the quantity value on a cart line. It becomes an editable field.
  2. Type the new quantity, or use the + and − buttons.
  3. Press Enter or click elsewhere to confirm. The line total updates automatically.

Removing an Item

  1. Click the × (delete) icon at the end of the cart line.
  2. The item is removed immediately from the cart.

Editing the Unit Price

  1. Click the unit price value on a cart line. It becomes an editable field.
  2. Enter the new price. This does not permanently change the product's catalogue price — it only affects this sale.
  3. Press Enter to confirm.
Editing the unit price below the purchase price will result in a negative margin. ShoeDZ will display a warning but will not prevent the sale. Use this capability carefully.

Per-Line Discount

  1. Click the discount icon (%) on a cart line.
  2. Enter a discount percentage (e.g., 10 for 10% off that line).
  3. Press Enter. The discounted line total is shown with the original price struck through.

Cart-Level Discount

  1. In the cart summary section at the bottom, locate the Cart Discount field.
  2. Enter a percentage discount to apply to the entire cart subtotal after any line-level discounts.
  3. The total updates in real time.
A cart-level discount is applied to the subtotal after per-line discounts, so the two stack multiplicatively, not additively.

3.6 Recording Payment

ShoeDZ supports five payment methods and allows split payment — the customer can pay with a combination of methods in a single transaction.

  1. Once the cart is complete, click Confirm Sale or press F10.
  2. The Payment dialog opens, showing the Amount Due.
  3. Select a payment method from the list below and enter the amount tendered for that method.
  4. To add a second (or third) payment method, click + Add Payment Method and repeat.
  5. The Remaining Balance indicator shows how much is still unpaid. It must reach 0.00 DZD before you can finalise.
  6. If cash is given and change is due, the Change amount is calculated and displayed automatically.
Payment MethodWhen to useExtra fields
Cash (Espèces) Physical banknotes and coins. Amount received (change calculated automatically).
Cheque Bank cheque from the customer. Cheque number, bank name, due date (post-dated cheques supported).
CCP Algeria Post (Algérie Poste) account transfer. Reference number (optional).
Virement Bancaire Bank wire transfer. Reference / transfer number (optional).
Account Balance Deduct from the client's credit balance (pre-paid account). Client must be selected for this session; available balance shown.
To use Account Balance, you must first associate the sale with a client. Click Assign Client in the cart panel before opening the payment dialog.
Cheques entered here are automatically added to the Cheque Tracker for follow-up. See Section 6.4 for details.

3.7 Confirming the Sale

  1. Once the total tendered equals the amount due, click Finalise Sale.
  2. ShoeDZ processes the transaction, deducts stock for each variant, and generates a unique Receipt Number (e.g., REC-2026-00847).
  3. A confirmation screen appears with two action buttons:
    • Print Receipt — Sends the receipt to the default printer. A thermal-printer-friendly layout is used automatically if a 58 mm or 80 mm receipt printer is set as the default.
    • Share via WhatsApp — Opens WhatsApp (web or app) with a pre-formatted receipt message and the client's phone number pre-filled (if a client was assigned).
  4. Click New Sale to start a fresh transaction, or Close to return to the Dashboard.
Once a sale is finalised, it cannot be deleted — only a return can reverse it. If you accidentally confirm a wrong sale, go to Sales History, locate the receipt, and process a return. This re-credits the stock.

3.8 Viewing Sale History

  1. From the sidebar, click Sales History.
  2. The list shows all completed sales, most recent first, with columns: Receipt Number, Date & Time, Cashier, Items, Total, Payment Methods.
  3. Use the filters at the top to narrow results:
    • Date range — Start and end date pickers.
    • Cashier — Filter by the user who made the sale.
    • Payment Method — Show only sales paid by a specific method.
    • Search — Enter a receipt number, product name, or client name.
  4. Click any row to open the full receipt detail — showing each line item, applied discounts, payment breakdown, and client (if assigned).
  5. From the receipt detail page, you can reprint the receipt or share it via WhatsApp again.

4.2 Product Variants and Barcodes

Each product can have multiple variants, combining EU shoe size and colour. Variants are what gets tracked in stock, scanned at the POS, and printed on barcode labels.

Adding a Variant

  1. On the product detail page, scroll to the Variants section.
  2. Click + Add Variant.
  3. Select the EU Size from the dropdown (28–47).
  4. Enter the Colour name (e.g., "Black", "White", "Red / White").
  5. Set the Initial Stock Quantity for this variant at the current branch.
  6. Set the Alert Threshold — when stock falls to or below this number, the variant appears in the Low Stock report and triggers a notification.
  7. A Barcode is auto-generated for the variant. You can override it with a supplier-provided EAN-13 barcode if desired.
  8. Click Save Variant.
You can add multiple variants at once using the Bulk Add option — click the arrow next to "+ Add Variant" and choose "Bulk Add". Enter a list of sizes separated by commas and a colour, and ShoeDZ will create all combinations.

Printing Barcode Labels

  1. On the product detail page, click the Print Labels button.
  2. In the label print dialog, select the specific variant(s) you want to print labels for.
  3. Enter the number of copies for each variant (e.g., print 6 labels if you received 6 pairs).
  4. Choose the label format (58 mm × 40 mm or 40 mm × 25 mm).
  5. Click Print. A print-ready PDF is generated and opened in a new tab. Send it to your label printer.
Labels include the product name, size, colour, price, and barcode. See Section 4.6 for how labels are automatically printed when receiving a stock transfer.

4.4 Stock Adjustments

Use stock adjustments to correct discrepancies between the system count and a physical count, or to write off damaged stock.

  1. From the Stock menu, click Adjustments.
  2. Click + New Adjustment.
  3. In the adjustment form, fill in:
    • Product Variant — Search and select the specific size and colour.
    • Branch — The location whose stock you are adjusting.
    • Quantity Delta — Enter a positive number to add stock, or a negative number to remove stock (e.g., −3 to write off 3 damaged pairs).
    • Reason — Select from: Physical Count, Damaged, Lost, Supplier Return, Other.
    • Notes — Free-text explanation (recommended for accountability).
  4. Click Save Adjustment. The stock level is updated immediately and a corresponding Stock Movement record is created.
Stock adjustments are irreversible. If you make an error, you must create a second adjustment to reverse it. All adjustments are visible in the Audit Log.

4.5 Low Stock Alerts

  1. From the Stock menu, click Low Stock.
  2. A list of all variants currently at or below their alert threshold is displayed.
  3. Each row shows: Product, Size, Colour, Branch, Current Stock, Alert Threshold.
  4. Click any row to navigate directly to that product's detail page where you can create a purchase order or adjust the alert threshold.
The "Low Stock SKUs" number on the Dashboard is a direct count of items on this page. If the number is climbing, it is time to review your reorder cadence.

4.6 Branch Transfers

If you operate multiple branches, use the transfer workflow to move stock between locations. Transfers maintain a full audit trail and ensure stock levels at both branches are accurately updated.

Creating a Transfer

  1. From the Stock menu, click Transfers.
  2. Click + New Transfer.
  3. Fill in the transfer form:
    • From Branch — The source branch sending the stock.
    • To Branch — The destination branch receiving the stock.
    • Product Variant — Select the specific size and colour.
    • Quantity — Number of pairs to transfer. Must not exceed available stock at the source branch.
    • Notes — Optional instructions for the driver or receiving staff.
  4. Click Create Transfer. The transfer is created with status Pending and stock at the source branch is immediately reserved (not yet deducted).

Receiving a Transfer

  1. At the destination branch, open Stock → Transfers.
  2. Find the transfer with status In Transit and click it.

If only part of the transfer arrives, you can receive a partial quantity. The remaining quantity stays In Transit until a second receipt action is performed.

5. Invoicing Pro Wholesale

The Invoicing module is available on the Pro Wholesale plan and above. It allows you to create professional Arabic-compliant invoices for B2B clients, track payments, and generate delivery notes and credit notes.

If you do not see the "Invoices" item in the sidebar, your current subscription plan does not include this module. Go to Settings → Billing to upgrade.

5.1 Creating an Invoice

  1. From the sidebar, click Invoices.
  2. Click + New Invoice.
  3. Fill in the invoice header:
    • Client — Search and select an existing client. The client's NIF, RC, and address are auto-filled. (Create the client first if they don't exist — see Section 6.1.)
    • Invoice Date — Defaults to today; adjust if needed.
    • Due Date — Payment deadline. Overdue status is triggered automatically after this date.
    • Series Prefix — Defaults to FA (from Facture). The number is auto-assigned sequentially (e.g., FA-2026-00001).
    • TVA Toggle — Enable to apply the Algerian standard TVA rate of 19% to all line items. The rate is shown on the invoice and in the totals sidebar.
    • Notes — Optional internal or client-facing notes printed at the bottom of the invoice.
  4. Click Save Draft to save without finalising, or continue to add line items.

5.2 Line Items and Totals

Adding Line Items

  1. In the Line Items section, click + Add Line.
  2. For each line, fill in:
    • Description — Free text, or click the product/variant selector icon to pull the description and price from your catalogue.
    • Quantity — Number of units.
    • Unit Price (HT) — Price before tax, in DZD.
    • Discount % — Optional per-line discount percentage.
  3. The Line Total HT is calculated automatically: (Qty × Unit Price) × (1 − Discount%).
  4. Repeat for all products or services on this invoice.
  5. To remove a line, click the × icon on that row.
  6. To reorder lines, drag and drop using the handle icon on the left of each row.

Totals Sidebar

5.3 Recording Invoice Payments

When a client makes a payment against an invoice, record it as follows:

  1. Open the invoice detail page.
  2. Scroll to the Payments section and click + Record Payment.
  3. Fill in the payment details:
    • Method — Cash, Cheque, CCP, Virement Bancaire, or Account Balance.
    • Amount — The amount paid in this instalment.
    • Date — The date the payment was received.
    • Reference — Optional cheque number, transfer reference, etc.
    • Notes — Optional.
  4. Click Save Payment. The Balance Due decreases by the payment amount.
  5. Repeat for additional instalments if the client is paying in multiple parts.
You can record multiple partial payments against a single invoice. The invoice status will automatically change to Partial after the first payment and Paid when the balance reaches zero.

  3. A professionally formatted A4 PDF is generated and downloaded. It includes:

    • Your company's NIF, RC, AI, address, and phone.

5.6 Delivery Notes (Bons de Livraison)

A delivery note accompanies a shipment and lists the goods physically delivered, without showing prices.

  1. Open the invoice to which this delivery relates.
  2. Click + Create Delivery Note.
  3. The line items from the invoice are pre-filled. Adjust quantities if only part of the order is being delivered now.
  4. Fill in:
    • Delivery Date — Date goods were delivered.
    • Delivered By — Name of the driver or logistics partner.
  5. Click Save Delivery Note. The note is auto-numbered (e.g., BL-2026-00032) and linked to the invoice.
  6. Download the PDF from the delivery note detail page. The PDF shows quantities and descriptions only — no prices.

5.7 Credit Notes (Avoirs)

A credit note (avoir) reduces or reverses an amount owed on an existing invoice — for example, due to a returned shipment or a pricing error.

  1. Open the original invoice.
  2. Click + Create Credit Note.
  3. Fill in:
    • Linked Invoice — Pre-filled with the current invoice number.
    • Reason — Free-text explanation (e.g., "3 pairs returned — wrong size").
    • Line Items — The credit note inherits the invoice's TVA rate. Add or modify lines to reflect the amount being credited.
  4. Click Save Credit Note. It is auto-numbered (e.g., AV-2026-00008).
  5. The credited amount is reflected in the client's ledger as a debit entry, reducing their outstanding balance.
Credit notes impact the client's running balance in the Ledger. See Section 6.2 — Client Detail Tabs.

Ledger Tab

7.2 Purchase Orders

Create a purchase order (PO) to formally document an order placed with a supplier.

  1. On the Suppliers list, click a supplier to open their detail page, then click + New Purchase Order. Alternatively, navigate to Suppliers → Purchase Orders → + New PO.
  2. Fill in the PO header:
    • Supplier — Pre-selected if opened from a supplier's page.
    • Expected Delivery Date — When you expect the goods to arrive.
    • Reference — Your internal PO number or the supplier's order reference (optional).
    • Destination Branch — Which branch will receive this stock.
    • Notes — Any special instructions for the supplier.
  3. In the Line Items section, click + Add Item for each product being ordered:
    • Variant — Select the product variant (name, size, colour), or type a free-text description for a non-catalogue item.
    • Quantity Ordered — Number of units ordered.
    • Purchase Price — Price per unit agreed with the supplier.
  4. Click Save Purchase Order. The PO is created with status Pending.
Saving a purchase order does not yet change any stock levels. Stock is only updated when the PO is received (Section 7.3).

7.3 Receiving a Purchase Order

When goods arrive at your branch, record the receipt against the purchase order to update stock levels.

  1. Open the purchase order (from Suppliers → Purchase Orders).
  2. Click Receive Goods.
  3. For each line item, enter the Received Quantity. If you received fewer than ordered (partial delivery), enter the actual received amount. You can receive the rest later.
  4. Enter any notes (e.g., "2 boxes arrived damaged — returned to driver").
  5. Click Confirm Receipt.
  6. Stock levels at the destination branch are updated immediately. A Reception stock movement is created for each line.
  7. If the purchase price on any line differs from the product's existing purchase price, ShoeDZ will ask if you want to update the product's purchase price to the new value. This affects future margin calculations.

Recording a Payment to a Supplier

9. Notifications

ShoeDZ's notification system proactively alerts you to events that require attention — overdue cheques, low stock, unpaid invoices, and general system messages — so you never miss a critical business event.

9.1 The Notification Bell

The bell icon (🔔) is located in the top bar on the right side, visible on every page of the application.

  • A red badge on the bell shows the count of unread notifications.
  • The bell refreshes automatically every 60 seconds — you do not need to reload the page to see new notifications.
  • Click the bell to open the notifications panel, which slides in from the right.

9.2 Notification Types

TypeColourTriggered When
Cheque Due Gold A receivable cheque's due date is within 3 days.
Low Stock Orange A product variant's stock falls to or below its alert threshold.
Invoice Overdue Red An invoice's due date has passed and the balance is still greater than zero.
General Blue System messages: new user invited, plan renewal, software updates.

9.3 Managing Notifications

  1. Click the bell icon to open the notifications panel.
  2. Notifications are listed with the most recent at the top. Each entry shows the type icon, a brief message, and a timestamp.
  3. Click any individual notification to mark it as read (the unread badge decreases). If the notification is linked to a record (e.g., a specific cheque or invoice), clicking it will also navigate you to that record.
  4. Click the "Mark all as read" button at the top of the panel to clear the entire unread count at once.
Make it a habit to check the notification bell at the start of each day. Red (Invoice Overdue) and Gold (Cheque Due) notifications in particular require timely action to protect your cash flow.
Notifications are per-user and per-role. Cashiers only receive General notifications. Managers and Owners receive all notification types.

10.6 Audit Log Manager+

The Audit Log is an immutable, tamper-proof record of every significant action taken in ShoeDZ. It is the foundation of your business's accountability and governance.

  2. A table of audit events is shown, newest first.
ColumnDescription
TimestampExact date and time the action occurred (server time, UTC+1 for Algeria).

Filtering the Audit Log

If you ever suspect unauthorised activity — a product price changed without authorisation, stock that went missing — start your investigation in the Audit Log. Filter by the affected model and the suspected time window to identify exactly who made the change and what the previous value was.
