## 8. Cash Drawer Movements (Cash In & Out) {#mouvements-caisse}

To track any non-sale cash drawer activity:

1. Press <kbd>Alt + M</kbd> or click **Drawer Movements**.
2. Select **Cash In** (adding change) or **Cash Out** (store expense).
3. Enter the exact amount and a mandatory reason. Confirm to open the drawer and print a slip.
