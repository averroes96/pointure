## 3. Registering Sales & Checkout {#encaisser}

The POS screen (<kbd>F1</kbd>) allows high-speed checkout using a barcode scanner or keyboard shortcuts.

### Adding Products
- **With a Barcode Scanner:** Scan the box barcode to immediately add the shoe with its matching size and colour to the cart.
- **With Manual Search (<kbd>F2</kbd>):** Press <kbd>F2</kbd>, type part of the item name or SKU, then select the size and colour.

### Payment Methods
- Cash (with automatic change calculation).
- Card / Electronic payment terminal (CIB / Edahabia).
- BaridiMob instant post transfer.
- Bank cheques and credit / customer accounts.
