## 6. Parking & Resuming Sales {#attente}

To serve the next customer in line without losing the current cart:

1. Press <kbd>Alt + H</kbd> or click **Park Sale**.
2. Type an identifying note (e.g., *"Lady in red coat"*) and confirm.
3. To resume, click the **Held Sales** badge (<kbd>Alt + H</kbd>) and click **Resume**.
