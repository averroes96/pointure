## 14. Promotional Campaigns and Customer Loyalty {#promotions-fidelite}

To drive foot traffic and encourage repeat store visits, ShoeDZ includes a promotions engine and customer loyalty points module.

### A. Creating a Seasonal Sale or Promotion

1. Navigate to the **Promotions** menu.
2. Click **+ New Promotional Campaign**.
3. Configure campaign parameters:
   - **Campaign Name:** e.g., *"Summer Sandals Sale"*, *"Back to School Special"*.
   - **Discount Type:** Percentage (e.g., `-20%`) or fixed amount in DZD (e.g., `-1 000 DZD`).
   - **Validity Window:** Start date/time and end date/time.
4. Define eligible items:
   - **Storewide:** Flat discount applied across the entire catalog.
   - **By Category or Department:** Kids' footwear only, or athletic trainers.
   - **Selected Models:** Clearance lines and end-of-series references.
5. Click **Activate Promotion**:
   - Once live, the discount applies automatically when scanning eligible items at checkout.
   - The printed receipt displays original price struck through, discount deduction, and net price charged.

### B. Customer Loyalty Points Program

ShoeDZ provides a transparent points-based loyalty rewards system:

1. **Enabling and Configuring the Program:**
   - Navigate to the **Customers → Loyalty** menu (`/loyalty`).
   - Enable and configure your loyalty program rules.
2. **Points Earning Ratio:**
   - Define the earning rule: for example, `1 loyalty point earned per 100 DZD spent`.
   - Each time an identified customer makes a purchase, their points balance increases automatically.
   - Accumulated points print at the foot of the receipt to incentivize return visits.
3. **Redeeming Points (Discount at Checkout):**
   - In accordance with ShoeDZ rules, **earned points are redeemed as a direct price discount on the checkout ticket**:
     - For example: `100 points = 500 DZD instant discount`.
   - When ringing up an eligible customer, the green **Redeem Loyalty Points** button illuminates.
   - In one click, the equivalent monetary discount is deducted from the ticket total, and corresponding points are deducted from the customer's account.
