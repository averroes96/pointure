# Cashier User Guide — Point of Sale {#guide-caisse}

This guide is designed for cashiers and sales clerks operating ShoeDZ at store counters. It walks you through every daily task from opening your drawer float to the end-of-day Z-Report closing.

---

## 1. System Login {#connexion}

To begin your cashier shift, sign in using your personal credentials.

> [!NOTE]
> Never operate under a colleague's account. Every sale, discount, and cash movement is audited under your user account.

### Login Steps
1. Enter your **mobile phone number** (10 digits starting with `05`, `06`, or `07`) or your email address.
2. Enter your **confidential password**.
3. Click **Sign in** (or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>).
