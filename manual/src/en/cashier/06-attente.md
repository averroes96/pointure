## 6. Parking and Resuming Sales {#attente}

When a shopper hesitates at the counter, steps back to try on another size, or leaves their wallet in the car, you shouldn't hold up the checkout line. The **Park Sale** (*Mise en attente*) feature allows you to set their cart aside and immediately serve the next customer.

### Parking an Active Sale (<kbd>Alt + H</kbd>)

1. While the customer's items are in the cart, click the **Park Sale** button at the bottom of the cart (or press <kbd>Alt + H</kbd>).
2. A dialog pops up to identify the ticket:
   - Type a short label or recognizable note (e.g., *"Customer in beige jacket"* or *"Black sneaker size 43"*).
3. Click **Confirm Park Sale**.
4. The cart clears immediately for the next customer, and an orange **Parked (1)** badge appears in the POS header.

### Resuming a Parked Ticket

1. Click on the orange **Parked** badge or press <kbd>Alt + H</kbd>.
2. The list of all currently held sales appears, displaying:
   - Time parked.
   - Identifying label or customer name.
   - Item count and total amount.
3. Click the green **Resume** button next to the desired ticket:
   - If your active cart is empty, the parked cart is fully restored (items, sizes, discounts, and selected customer).
   - The ticket is removed from the held sales list.
4. You can now add more items or proceed directly to checkout (<kbd>F10</kbd>).

> [!NOTE]
> **Safety and System Rules:**
> - Each store branch can maintain a maximum of **20 parked tickets** simultaneously.
> - If a parked sale is not retrieved within **24 hours**, it is automatically purged by nightly system maintenance to prevent inventory reservation drift.
