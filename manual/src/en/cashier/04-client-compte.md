## 4. Customer Management and Credit Sales {#client-compte}

By default, over-the-counter sales are rung up under a generic *Walk-in Customer*. To foster customer loyalty or extend deferred credit payment, attach their customer profile to the sale ticket.

### Selecting an Existing Customer

1. In the upper right area of the cart, click on the **Customer** field (or press <kbd>Alt + V</kbd>).
2. Type the customer's name or the first digits of their mobile phone number.
3. Click the customer from the list: their name and current outstanding balance appear on the cart banner.

### Quickly Creating a New Customer at Checkout

If the customer is not yet registered in your store database:
1. Click the `+ New Customer` button next to the customer search bar.
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Full Name** or business name.
   - **Mobile Phone Number** (standard Algerian mobile format starting with `05`, `06`, or `07`).
   - **Wilaya** of residence among the 58 Algerian wilayas.
3. Click **Save**: the new customer is automatically assigned to the active cart.

### Credit Sales (On Customer Account)

If store management allows the customer to take their footwear and settle payment at a later date:

1. Ensure the customer profile is attached to the cart (credit sales are strictly prohibited for a *Walk-in Customer*).
2. Click **Checkout** (<kbd>F10</kbd>).
3. From the list of payment methods, select **Customer Account / Credit**.
4. The system automatically verifies the customer's **Credit Limit** established by the manager:
   - If the new balance exceeds the authorized limit, the sale is blocked or prompts for manager approval.
5. Confirm the sale: the customer's outstanding balance increases by the purchase amount, and the printed receipt displays a *Credit Sale* notice.

> [!TIP]
> When the customer returns days later to settle their debt, record their payment directly from their file in the **Customers** menu (<kbd>F4</kbd>) by clicking **New Payment**.
