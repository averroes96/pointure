## 8. Cash Drawer Movements (Cash In & Out) {#mouvements-caisse}

During the working day, cash may be added to or withdrawn from the drawer outside of regular sales transactions (for example: breaking change with a neighbouring shop, paying for courier delivery, or purchasing store cleaning supplies).

> [!NOTE]
> Any physical movement of cash must be logged in the software. Otherwise, your evening closing will show an unexplained cash discrepancy.

### Recording Cash In or Cash Out (<kbd>Alt + M</kbd>)

1. On the POS screen, press the keyboard shortcut <kbd>Alt + M</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl + M</kbd>), or click the **Cash Movements** button.
2. In the dialog, select the operation type:
   - **Cash In (Deposit / Float top-up):** Adding money into the drawer (e.g., coin rolls of 100 DZD or 200 DZD for change).
   - **Cash Out (Withdrawal / Expense):** Removing money from the drawer (e.g., settling a coffee invoice, buying packing bags, salary advances, or handing over an interim cash pickup to the store owner).
3. Enter the **Exact Amount** in DZD.
4. Provide a mandatory **Reason or Explanatory Note** (e.g., *"Purchased packing bags 1 500 DZD"*).
5. Click **Confirm Movement**.
6. The cash drawer opens to perform the physical cash transaction, and a receipt voucher prints. Place this receipt in the drawer alongside any vendor purchase receipts.

### Viewing Drawer Cash Balance

At any point during the day, the session banner informs you of:
- Starting cash float.
- Total cash received from sales.
- Total recorded cash in and cash out movements.
- **Expected Theoretical Balance** currently in the drawer.
