## 2. Daily Cash Drawer Opening {#ouvrir-caisse}

Each work day or sales shift begins with the official opening of your cash register session. This step records the exact starting physical cash present in the drawer (cash float).

> [!WARNING]
> You cannot record any sales transactions until your cash register session has been opened.

### Steps to Open the Cash Drawer

1. Navigate to the **POS & Sales** screen (<kbd>F1</kbd>) or click on the **Open Drawer** warning banner.
2. In the dialog that opens, physically count the bills and coins present in the cash drawer.
3. Enter the exact **Starting cash float** in Algerian Dinars (DZD) (e.g., `5 000 DZD`).
4. Optionally, add a brief note (e.g., *"Float supplied by store manager"*).
5. Click **Confirm Drawer Opening**.

> [!TIP]
> Counting your starting cash float down to the cent prevents false cash discrepancies during the evening closing (Z-Report).
