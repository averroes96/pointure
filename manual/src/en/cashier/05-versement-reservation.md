## 5. Down Payments and Layaway Reservations {#versement-reservation}

In footwear retail, customers often wish to reserve a high-demand shoe or rare size by placing a down payment (*Arboun* or deposit), returning later to settle the remaining balance and take their box.

### Creating a Layaway Reservation with a Down Payment

1. Add the selected pair of shoes to the cart with the exact size and colour.
2. Select the named **Customer** profile (<kbd>Alt + V</kbd>).
3. Click the **Down Payment / Reservation** button.
4. Enter the deposit amount handed over by the customer (e.g., `2 000 DZD` for a pair priced at `7 500 DZD`).
5. Specify the agreed **Pickup Deadline** (e.g., within 7 days or by month end).
6. Click **Confirm Reservation**.
7. The system deducts the pair from available shelf inventory and moves it to reserved stock, then prints a **Deposit Receipt** detailing the amount paid, the remaining balance due, and the reservation reference number. Hand this receipt to the customer.

### Pickup and Settling the Remaining Balance

When the customer returns to the store to collect their reserved shoes:

1. Open the **Down Payments / Reservations** tab or look up the customer in the Customers menu (<kbd>F4</kbd>).
2. Click on the corresponding reservation record.
3. Review the remaining balance due (e.g., `5 500 DZD`).
4. Click **Collect Remaining Balance**.
5. Select the payment method (cash, TPE, etc.) and confirm.
6. The final sales receipt prints, the status updates to *Delivered / Settled*, and you can hand over the shoe box to the customer.

---

### Cancelling a Reservation

If the customer does not return before the deadline or decides not to proceed with the purchase:

1. Open the relevant reservation file.
2. Click **Cancel Reservation**.
3. Choose whether the deposited amount is:
   - **Refunded in cash to the customer** (requires Manager PIN authorization).
   - **Retained as store credit (Avoir)** on the customer's account.
4. Confirm: the pair of shoes is automatically returned to available shelf stock for resale.
