## 5. Layaway & Deposit Installments {#versement-reservation}

To hold an item for a customer with an advance deposit:

1. Add the selected pair of shoes to the cart and select the named customer (<kbd>Alt + V</kbd>).
2. Click **Layaway / Deposit**, enter the down payment amount and the pickup deadline.
3. When the customer returns, open their reservation record, collect the remaining balance, and hand over the item with the final receipt.
